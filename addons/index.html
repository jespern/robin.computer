Add-ons

Pick what Robin
can do for you.

Each add-on says what it can reach, the keys it needs, how often it may be used and the memory and CPU it may use. Robin enforces all of it. Built-in add-ons come with Robin; switch them on in the app. The others install from this list, in the app under Add-ons or from the terminal, after you review exactly what they may do.

    Robin

    Robin's own knowledge: your mail, calendar, people, facts, open loops and its decisions. Always on.

    Can reach
    Nothing outside your machine
    14 tools
    • search Search everything Robin holds: mail and calendar events across accounts and providers, people (kind:person: name, addresses, relationship and your notes), and your earlier conversation with Robin (kind:chat), offline and fast; best match first, recent first among equals.
    • read_thread Read one local thread by its Robin thread_id.
    • get_message Read one local message by Robin message_id, including available capped plain text.
    • query_calendar Read local occurrences between from and to (dates and times; without an offset they are the owner's local time), maximum 90 days.
    • explain_decision Read a recorded decision and provenance for one Robin message_id.
    • facts Read the owner's facts: typed key-value pairs such as passport.expiry or passport.issuing_country, with where each came from.
    • query_loops List open loops: requests the owner sent and waits on (kind waiting), replies the owner owes (owed) and promises the owner made (promised), each with the other side, what was asked, since when, topics such as passport_renewal, when Robin follows it up and whether that is overdue.
    • propose_fact Propose saving one fact about the owner, such as passport.expiry 2028-06-14, when the owner states it or you read it in their mail.
    • schedule_reminder Remind the owner of something later, once or on a repeat: a push and a card in Chat at that time, with Done and Snooze.
    • schedule_task Have Robin do something later or on a schedule, when the owner asks for that ("every Monday morning, check …", "tomorrow at 8, look up …"): at each time Robin runs a turn of its own with instruction as what to do, using its read tools, and its answer appears in Chat with a push.
    • list_reminders List the owner's reminders and scheduled tasks still to come (and with include_finished, recent done and cancelled ones): id, text, the schedule in words, state (active, paused, fired: waiting for Done) and next times.
    • cancel_reminder Cancel one of the owner's reminders by its id from list_reminders, when the owner asks.
    • find_person Find people the owner deals with by name, email address, relationship or part of any: each with the owner's name for them, relationship and notes, their addresses, first and last contact, how many mails and calendar events they appear in, and recent threads (use read_thread).
    • propose_person Propose a change to a person for the owner to save: their name, relationship (e.g.

    Always on in Robin.

    Maps

    Travel times with traffic, and places you mention: address, phone, website and opening hours.

    Can reach
    routes.googleapis.com, places.googleapis.com, maps.googleapis.com
    Needs
    Google maps key
    Monthly limits
    Route requests: 500 a month; Place lookups: 250 a month; Map images: 100 a month, yours to change
    Runs with
    128 MB memory · 1 CPU · up to 32 processes
    2 tools
    • travel_time Travel time between two places from Google Maps, with predicted traffic when driving: for planning a trip, a school run or when to leave.
    • find_place Look up a place on Google Maps, such as a restaurant, venue, shop or school the owner mentions: name, address, phone, website, opening hours, whether it is open now and its status (closed for good, temporarily closed).

    In Robin: Add-ons, then switch it on.

  • Built in · Robin

    Phone

    Robin's phone line: call Robin from an allowed number, and Robin places calls for you. Every call is in Calls.

    Can reach
    api.openai.com
    Needs
    OpenAI key, SIP password
    Monthly limits
    Minutes of calls Robin places: 60 a month; Voice minutes of practice calls a month (both sides count): 300 a month, yours to change
    7 tools
    • propose_call Propose a phone call Robin places for the owner, for example to book a table or ask a business a question.
    • propose_calls Propose 2 to 10 calls for one errand together (for example asking several shops the same question): each item is a propose_call brief.
    • propose_text Propose a text (SMS) Robin sends for the owner from one of Robin's US numbers.
    • recent_texts Text conversations on Robin's numbers, newest first: the other number, which of Robin's numbers, the latest text and how many are unread.
    • read_texts One text conversation by number from recent_texts: the texts both ways, oldest first, with what Robin sent and whether it went.
    • recent_calls The calls Robin answered or placed, newest first: who, when, how long, how it ended and, for calls Robin placed, the result.
    • read_call One call by call_id from recent_calls: its summary, what was agreed, follow-ups, the brief it was placed from, and the transcript.

    In Robin: Add-ons, then switch it on.

    Browser

    A confined browser for research and errands Robin runs for you; never used from chat directly.

    Can reach
    api.browser-use.com, *.cdp.browser-use.com
    Needs
    Browser use key
    Monthly limits
    Browser actions: 1,000 a month, yours to change
    Runs with
    128 MB memory · 1 CPU · up to 32 processes
    6 tools
    • browser_open Open an http or https address in this run's browser and read its first part.
    • browser_read Read the current page again, or a later part of it (part 2, 3, …).
    • browser_click Click element n from the last read, then read the page it leads to.
    • browser_type Type text into text field n from the last read, replacing what is there; submit presses Enter.
    • browser_select Choose an option, by its visible text, in list n from the last read.
    • browser_back Go back to the previous page and read it.

    In Robin: Add-ons, then switch it on.

    Remote images in mail

    Can reach

    Monthly limits
    Images: 20,000 a month, yours to change
    Runs with
    128 MB memory · 1 CPU · up to 32 processes
    3 tools
    • fetch_image One remote image an email shows, fetched over https without cookies or referrer.
    • fetch_logo One brand logo (an SVG) a sender's domain publishes for BIMI, fetched over https without cookies or referrer.
    • find_icon The icon a sender's site declares on its homepage (<link rel="icon">), as a URL only.

    In Robin: Add-ons, then switch it on.
